Privacy Policy - Seerah Journey

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Seerah Journey (package com.seerahjourney.app) is an Islamic learning app developed by Daniel Armi. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, sign-ups, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and progress (language, selected reciter, night mode, course and quiz progress) are saved only on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Third-party content

To play Quran recitation and translation audio, the app streams audio files from EveryAyah.com, and map screens load map tiles from Esri. These services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when content is requested, as with any website. Please see their own privacy policies for details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the developer through the Google Play Store listing.
